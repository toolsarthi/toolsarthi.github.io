Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ToolSarthi.

General Questions

1. What is ToolSarthi?

ToolSarthi is an online tools website that provides useful tools for working with PDF files, images and other digital tasks.

2. Are ToolSarthi tools free?

Our tools are intended to be easy to access. Please check the individual tool page for any specific limitations or requirements.

3. Do I need to install software?

Most tools are designed to work through your web browser. Some functions may depend on your browser and device.

4. Can I use ToolSarthi on my mobile phone?

Yes, you can open ToolSarthi on a mobile phone using a modern web browser. The experience may vary depending on your device.

PDF Tools

5. How do I use a PDF tool?

Open the PDF tool you need, select your file, follow the instructions shown on the page, and download the result if the tool completes successfully.

6. Why is my PDF not processing?

Check that your file is a supported PDF, try a smaller file, and make sure your browser has finished loading the page. If the problem continues, contact us.

7. Why is my downloaded file missing?

Check your browser's Downloads section and your device's Downloads folder. Also check whether your browser asked you to choose a download location.

Image Tools

8. How do I edit an image?

Open the image tool you want to use, select an image, adjust the available settings, and follow the instructions on that tool's page.

9. Which image formats are supported?

Supported formats depend on the individual tool and your browser. Check the tool page before selecting your image.

10. Why is my image taking a long time to process?

Large images may take longer to process, especially on mobile devices. Try using a smaller image and keep the page open until processing finishes.

Privacy and Security

11. Are my files stored on ToolSarthi?

File handling depends on how each tool is implemented. Please read our Privacy Policy and avoid using sensitive files unless you understand how the specific tool processes them.
